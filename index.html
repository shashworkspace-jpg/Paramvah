Terms & Conditions

Please read these project terms carefully before signing the agreement.

  1. Once the agreement is signed, no change in the agreement is accepted.
  2. The Owner/Buyer shall not issue any change order or other directive requiring additional compensable work to be performed under this contract, which work causes the aggregate amount payable under the contract to exceed the appropriated amount for the original contract, unless the Contractor/Builder is given written assurance by the Owner that lawful appropriations to cover the costs of the additional work have been made, or unless such work is covered under a remedy-granting provision in the contract.
  3. The builder should not extend the time of completion of the project beyond 8 months, subject to the payments given by the first party.
  4. All temporary connection costs and monthly bills are to be borne by the buyer/client.
  5. The buyer is to obtain and pay for all necessary permits and licenses relative to the project.
  6. The attached working plan of the building must be attested by both parties, and no deviation from the working drawing can be made by the builder.
  7. Any work related to elevation charges will be additional, including MS fabrication, utility grill, Fundermax, stone cladding, etc. Elevation work includes only civil work, wall tiles of ₹50/sq. ft. up to 100 sq. ft./floor, and texture paint up to 200 sq. ft./floor.
  8. Any work outside the site area, i.e. electrical earthing and drainage linking to the chamber, will be additional.
  9. Any additional provisions needed should be discussed with the project in-charge, and an estimate should be obtained from the builder as per the changes or requirements.
  10. All materials used in the construction are of the best quality and may vary as per conditional requirements.
  11. Any additional work-related charges should be cleared or settled within one month or at the time of purchase of the material.
  12. The builder/second party promises to complete all works honestly as mentioned in the agreement.
  13. Where two brands are mentioned for any particulars, materials from whichever brand is in stock in the market at that time may be used.
  14. Any deviations from the above agreement or missing statements will be charged on a pro-rata basis.
  15. Solar provision will be made for each house, but solar fitting materials such as G.I. pipe, G.I. union, etc., and labour charges will be additional.
  16. The buyer has the right to visit and inspect the materials at any time without prior notification.
  17. Any complaints or clarifications will be between the buyer and builder only.
  18. Any changes requested by the buyer can be made during construction. Once the work is completed, no alteration or demolition will be done. Charges will apply for any deviations from the agreed plan or design.
